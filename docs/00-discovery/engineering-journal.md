# Engineering Journal

## Purpose

This journal is the running log of architecture and product discovery discussions for the Engineering Intelligence Platform. It captures the context, questions, emerging insights, and unresolved topics that shape the platform before those ideas are finalized into approved documentation or Architecture Decision Records.

Use this journal to preserve exploration without prematurely turning it into a decision. When a topic matures into a durable choice, it should be promoted to the relevant design document or ADR rather than treated as settled here.
